AeroComfort

Bring first-class comfort for economy pricing

Airplane seats are built for efficiency, not for the human spine. AeroComfort is a back cushion engineered specifically for airplane seats, so you can sleep in a natural posture and step off a long flight without the soreness.

It folds down to the size of a small book, expands into a full cushion system when you need it, and clips onto your carry-on. We made it because we fly long-haul between three continents every year, and nothing on the market fixed our backs.

Problem

Uncomfortable seats. Arriving tired before your trip even begins. And worst of all, back pain that lingers long after you land.

Back pain and posture

Sitting in stiff chairs for hours, especially non-reclining ones, puts strain on the lower spine that a neck pillow never reaches.

Sleep deprivation

Many of our classmates told us they struggle to sleep at all on flights home that run ten hours or more.

Discomfort

Cramped economy seating with nothing built to help. Most travel products only focus on the neck and ignore where discomfort actually begins.

The pillows airlines hand out don’t solve these problems. They don’t even attempt to.

Real
support

AeroComfort is the first portable mini mattress engineered for airplane seats, and it is adaptive to everyone.

It aligns and supports the lower spine against rigid seats, and it allows for countless comfortable sitting positions instead of locking you into one. Our goal was a one-size-fits-all design that matches the dimensions of a plane seat and is still compact enough to carry.

What comes in the kit

  • Adjustable long memory-foam cushion that expands on contact with air
  • Adjustable back braces, longer or firmer, for posture
  • A separate leg brace, because legs need different support than backs
  • Integrated mesh system that attaches securely to your carry-on

Memory foam that lasts

High-quality, durable memory foam gives the balance of support and softness. It’s fully reusable and built to last, so it pays for itself across trips.

Folds to book size

The collapsible design fits easily into a purse or backpack, combining strong structure with real portability, something most large cushions lack.

Full-body support

Back and leg support, not just the neck. That is what keeps your posture healthy over a long flight.

Planes, trains, cars, home

Use it on long drives, train rides or on the couch. It’s more versatile than a single-purpose neck pillow.

Designed with chiropractors

Developed in collaboration with chiropractors to help your spine sit straight and comfortably, which is nearly impossible in an airplane seat.

Rest, then arrive ready

Arrive feeling rested and pain-free, ready to start your trip. Your flight should be as enjoyable as the destination.

Who
we are

Questions

Will it fit my seat?

AeroComfort is designed as one size fits all and matched to the dimensions of a standard airplane seat, including stiff, non-reclining economy seats.

How small does it pack?

It folds down to roughly the size of a small book, fits in a purse or backpack, and attaches to your carry-on with the integrated mesh system.

Is it only for planes?

No. It works on trains, in cars and at home. Planes are just where we needed it most.

How is it different from a neck pillow?

Neck pillows support your head. AeroComfort supports your lower back and legs, where posture problems on long flights actually start.

When can I buy one?

We are refining the prototype and identifying a manufacturing partner before deciding on a launch timeline.
